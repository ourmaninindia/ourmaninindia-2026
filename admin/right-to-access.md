In practice someone emails you saying "what data do you hold about me?" and you:

1. Check Netlify Forms for any comments or contact form submissions with their email
2. Check Brevo for their newsletter subscription and preferences
3. Reply with what you found

You don't need an automated system. The GDPR right to access was designed with large companies in mind — for a personal blog a manual check and email reply within 30 days (the legal requirement) is perfectly sufficient.

The same applies to the right to erasure — someone asks you to delete their data, you delete their Netlify form submissions and/or Brevo contact, and confirm by email.

The only thing worth adding to your contact form is a dropdown or note so people can indicate it's a GDPR request — makes it easier to identify and prioritise. But even that is optional.
