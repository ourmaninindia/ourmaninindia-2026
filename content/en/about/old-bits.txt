What truly drives me? **Lifelong learning.**  
Research shows that performance in the workplace is shaped by four key factors: competencies, experiences, traits, and drivers. These are not only predictive of performance differences but also strongly correlate with essential outcomes such as engagement, retention, productivity, and leadership potential.
The cross-cultural training courses I’ve led often centered around the same question ethologists ask: **why do we behave the way we do?**

Cooperation and competition exist in both nature and socio-economic systems. It’s not the strongest species that survives, nor the most intelligent—but the one most adaptable to change.  
At the heart of adaptability is learning and growth. The critical question we must ask—whether as individuals or organizations—is:  
**Does our rate of internal growth exceed the rate of external change?**
