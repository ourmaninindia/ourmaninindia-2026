I am Dutch, so I have cycled all my life. As a child it was the way to explore, to seek adventure.There are ample reasons why a bike can stay in the garage: too busy, too dangerous, too hot, too cold.

Cycling has been meditation, freedom, and perspective. On projects across the Netherlands, India, and beyond, it helped me clear my mind and see things differently. A humble bicycle can spark curiosity, surprise, and connection.

I have cycled the Stelvio, Col d’Iseran, and Mont Ventoux with groups. I raced locally in Goa and desperately tried to keep pace with Swift riders in Leiden and my cycling buddy in IJmuiden. I explored  solo in Portugal, the Balkans, Kazahkstan, Mauritius, and Thailand.

Every ride brings new scenery, challengess, and encounters. In some countries I could leave my bike  outside a shop unlocked — a trust and simplicity rare in the Netherlands..

Cycling sharpens the mind, deepens appreciation for people and nature, and enriches life. that is what keeps me going.
